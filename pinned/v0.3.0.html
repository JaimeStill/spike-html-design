spike-html-design

The baseline's tokens, every element its elements layer styles, and its tools, rendered by baseline.css alone. It follows the system scheme; a data-scheme of light or dark on the root element forces one.

This page is a workbench. Its arrangement, the full-width grids, the dashed cells and their labels, is composition, in the page's own specimen layer after the baseline: it shows how a composition arranges within the design system, and none of it is the baseline. What sits inside each cell, under its label, is the baseline alone.

    Contrast

    Contrast is graded where legibility matters: text over a color. Each matrix, one per scheme, grades every text token and hue, its rows, over every surface, its columns, by the WCAG 2 ratio from relative luminance. AA meets 4.5 for body text; AA large meets 3 for large text; fail is below 3. Each mark is judged on the unrounded ratio, so a 4.50 can still be AA large and a 3.00 can still fail. Surfaces get no ratio of their own. Failures are recorded here, not corrected.
